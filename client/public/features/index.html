Frequently asked questions

Which features do I get for my business type?

When you register you choose a company type. SKPOS switches on the modules that type needs (for example Serial / IMEI and Warranty for a Mobile Store, the Liquor Shop module for a wine shop, Vehicle Service for a service centre). Anything else included in your package can be switched on in Configurations.

Can I use one login for several branches?

Yes. Add branches and warehouses in Configurations › Branch & Locations. Stock is kept per location, users can be limited to their own locations, and Transfer Orders move stock between them with approvals.

Does SKPOS work on a phone or tablet?

Yes, it runs in any modern browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox — on a PC, laptop, tablet or phone. A barcode scanner that types like a keyboard works on every billing screen.

Is GST handled?

Yes. Items carry HSN / SAC and GST rate; bills split CGST + SGST or IGST automatically from the customer's state, and the Sales and Purchase reports include rate-wise GST and HSN summaries for GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.

How do I give credit and collect later?

Choose a saved customer and pay with “Credit (On Account)”. The amount shows in Sales › Outstanding Collection & Refunds, where you collect against the bills, keep advances, adjust them to new bills or refund them.

Where is my data kept?

Your data is stored in a secure cloud database, separate for every company. Owners can export any report to CSV at any time.
